PAL-only timing implementation: hardware-confirmed on 2026-10-26

CoCoTANG-pal-analog.gprj replaces two sources from the original shared project.
RGB/YPbPr keep CoCoTANG-pal.gprj; CVBS keeps its approved project and sources.
CPU, SDRAM, SSC, IDE, GIME, IRQ and HDMI logic are unchanged.

The analog path uses the approved CVBS counter/read-address and RGB222 lookup
improvements. Composite smoothing is disabled; NTSC field alignment is off.
PAL DDS frequency, alternating V phase, swinging burst and S-Video waveform
are preserved. Existing PAL-chroma/machine-clock behavior is unchanged.

The current PAL encoder selects line inversion with carrier phase bits and
uses five-bit chroma sign arithmetic with exact fractional rounding. It
preserves the original eleven-bit wrap and register latency. All 65536 signed
operand/phase combinations match the original arithmetic; full waveform
comparisons cover both raster standards, native/compatibility geometry and
192/199/225-line modes. PAL carrier/line inversion tests pass.

build_options.json preserves the previous PAL placement settings: place1,
route1, replication=true, maxfan16. No other builds load this configuration.
