Combined HDMI and analog RGB pixel timing candidate, 2026-11-06.
Not yet hardware confirmed. All confirmed stable images remain unchanged.

HDMI's mask was delayed one clock while cache data took two clocks.
That repeated the first column and dropped the final column. In the RGB
build only, delay the mask by both cache stages and extend the RAM read
window by one clock to return the final pending address. Clamp that flush
request to the last address; it does not add a displayed column.
Native video retains all 640 columns; compatibility retains all 512.
The centered HDMI picture edges are 40/680 and 104/616 respectively.
Line/field timing and prefetch scheduling are unchanged.

Retains analog RGB fix from rgb-pixel-width-test: latch cache pixels once
per sample to prevent read-ahead from shortening the first native dot.

All clocks meet routed timing; clk108 Fmax 108.006 MHz and clk135 Fmax
135.136 MHz. Zero setup/hold violations. The clk108 margin is extremely
narrow. Project, placement, routing, synthesis and clock settings unchanged.

Targeted tests pass:
- Every HDMI column, both edges, doubled row copies and cache-bank changes,
  native/compatibility at 50/60 Hz, normal colours and both artifact palettes.
  Covers real wrapper/cache and encoder RGB capture. Physical serializer
  stubbed; DVI video branch used for Icarus audio-array compatibility.
- Analog RGB widths through inverse LUT: 640 x 10 and 256 x 20 clocks.
- RGB alternating DAC phases and HDMI video-standard transitions.

The full suite was not repeated; earlier pre-existing failures are listed
in rgb-pixel-width-test-notes.txt and its saved regression log.

make program-rgb programs this combined candidate. The earlier analog-only
candidate remains rgb-pixel-width-test.fs. Confirm video and Boink before
promoting either candidate over stable-rgb.fs.

REJECTED after user reported analog noise. User confirmed "yeah no noise"
with rgb-hdmi-only-noise-test.fs. Analog sample-latching change rolled back;
